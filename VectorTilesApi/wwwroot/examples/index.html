This dataset contains comprehensive pedestrian trail network data including hiking trails, mountain paths, alpine routes, and other pedestrian pathways across the cross-border alpine region. The dataset includes trail difficulty classifications, closure information fields for dynamic updates, and multilingual attributes supporting the trilingual nature of the EUREGIO region. Data is produced under the DIGIWAY project.

Digiway Euregio Examples

EUREGIO Transport Networks (FC1 road network, FC2 hiking routes) filtered by the tags assigned on import. Region and category are combined with tagfilter=and(...).

  • EUREGIO Hiking Paths - Euregio Hiking paths (FC2 hiking routes and FC1 network hiking trails, tag hiking) in South Tyrol, North Tyrol and Trentino. Filter tagfilter=hiking, points clustered, tracks from zoom level 10.

  • EUREGIO Bike Routes - Euregio Bicycle/pedestrian paths of the FC1 road network (tag cycling). The source contains no bike routes for Trentino in South Tyrol, North Tyrol and Trentino. Filter tagfilter=cycling, points clustered, tracks from zoom level 10.
  • EUREGIO Bike Routes - South Tyrol Bicycle/pedestrian paths of the FC1 road network (tag cycling). The source contains no bike routes for Trentino in South Tyrol. Filter tagfilter=and(cycling,south tyrol), points clustered, tracks from zoom level 10.
  • EUREGIO Bike Routes - North Tyrol Bicycle/pedestrian paths of the FC1 road network (tag cycling). The source contains no bike routes for Trentino in North Tyrol. Filter tagfilter=and(cycling,tyrol), points clustered, tracks from zoom level 10.

  • EUREGIO Forestry Access Roads - Euregio Forestry access roads of the FC1 road network (tag forestry access road) in South Tyrol, North Tyrol and Trentino. Filter tagfilter=forestry access road, points clustered, tracks from zoom level 10.
